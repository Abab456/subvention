# ÉcoPerformance — selected streams

Checked 2026-11-15 America/Toronto; review recorded 2026-11-16T04:42:16Z. Metadata: **OFFICIAL_PAGE_REVIEWED**. Full framework/formula review: **NOT_VERIFIED**.

The [official overview](https://www.quebec.ca/agriculture-environnement-et-ressources-naturelles/energie/reussir-ses-projets-transition-energetique/aide-financiere/programme-ecoperformance) states a framework effective **2026-07-07** and closure of **new RCx requests from 2026-09-01**. This does not close every stream. The engine needs new-versus-existing application context; existing files are reviewed separately. The closure claim is bounded to this research snapshot.

The [analysis stream](https://www.quebec.ca/agriculture-environnement-et-ressources-naturelles/energie/reussir-ses-projets-transition-energetique/aide-financiere/programme-ecoperformance/analyse-mesures-reduction-ges) concerns Québec organizations/sites with fossil-fuel or relevant process/fugitive-emission context, and requires an engineering study. It distinguishes analysis classes, eligible expenses and commitment timing. Public rates/caps alone are insufficient to approve an estimate; preparation-of-application expenses are excluded on this page.

The [standard implementation stream](https://www.quebec.ca/agriculture-environnement-et-ressources-naturelles/energie/reussir-ses-projets-transition-energetique/aide-financiere/programme-ecoperformance/projet-implantation-standard) uses engineering-dependent baseline/additionality, eligible costs, avoided emissions, engagement period and payback criteria. It distinguishes measures belonging to simplified implementation. It is not a generic percentage of a renovation invoice.

Review inputs: applicant/site, activity/use, energy class and consumption, fossil/process baseline, study/measure class, professional evidence, commitment/application dates, eligible costs and other assistance. No formulas are transcribed or implemented. The normative framework and stream/stacking details need full review; all figures are null and stacking UNKNOWN. Measures outside these three selected streams receive no claimed match in this catalog.
