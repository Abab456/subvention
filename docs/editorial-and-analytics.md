# Authority, editorial operations and measurement

Checked November 16, 2026. Public acquisition is gated; the current app is noindex and loopback-only. No external analytics/search account has been created or activated.

## Editorial system

The versioned business catalog is the source for program pages, source URLs, checked/effective dates and distinct metadata/calculation states. A reviewed official description does not validate eligibility or a formula. Each substantive update should carry a source/version, bounded paraphrase, affected rule/content IDs, editor, technical reviewer where needed and approval record in its PR. Proposed states: research → source verification → technical/editorial review → approved draft → founder-authorized publication. Current pages are reviewable drafts, not proof of a live service.

Source monitoring emits hashed before/after evidence and pending-human-approval proposals. Its CLI and workflow cannot modify the authority catalog or publish. Failed retrieval is not program closure. An approved baseline only establishes what was captured, not correctness. AI may propose research summaries or copy; a named reviewer must inspect sources and material changes before publication. Do not reproduce official guides or mass-generate pages from keyword lists.

Content priorities: business program catalog and specific participation guides; a pre-commitment checklist; commercial HVAC/controls/refrigeration and heat-recovery evidence requirements; Énergir study-led projects; engineered industrial baselines; how to distinguish a grant, financing, a public cap, an approved decision and a cash receipt. Sector resources should answer real workflow questions from interviews. Current application includes program pages and the pre-commitment guide; a larger editorial library is not claimed complete.

French is the primary editorial language; maintain a professional English counterpart with the same evidence and uncertainty. Use descriptive stable URLs, meaningful server HTML, unique titles/descriptions, internal links, canonical/hreflang only with an approved configured origin, a reviewed sitemap and appropriate structured data. Never invent an official affiliation, testimonials, ratings, prices, location or an indexed production domain. Nonproduction and private records must remain noindex.

Reference: [Google Search Central SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide). Indexable architecture does not guarantee indexing or ranking.

## Measurement decision

R1 stores optional allowlisted first-party event counts only after a separate affirmative analytics choice. No personal identity, IP, referrer URL, user-agent, fingerprint, raw URL/query or arbitrary properties are persisted in the event table. IP-based abuse counters are ephemeral memory and separate from analytics. Service-request consent is not analytics, marketing or partner-sharing consent. Withdrawing analytics consent stops later optional events. Operational records/audit follow their service purpose and must receive an appropriate retention policy before real use.

Available now: assessment event counts when opted in; persisted leads/cases; quote acceptance; draft fees; verified synthetic covered receipts; zero cash revenue because collection is disabled. These are synthetic development metrics. They do not measure organic traffic, paid customer conversions, acquisition cost or actual revenue. Do not present a draft invoice or customer grant as collected platform revenue.

After public launch approval, start with Search Console for verified-site search impressions, clicks, queries and indexing coverage, plus internal operational outcomes. [Google's search help](https://developers.google.com/search/help) describes Search Console's role. No account or verification token has been installed here.

Consider GA4 only if campaign attribution/experimentation requirements justify it after privacy/vendor/consent review. [Google's consent-mode documentation](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10000067) distinguishes storage controls and pings: do not assume that configuring a consent flag means no data is transmitted. Default-deny loading/transmission, validated withdrawal and a reviewed retention/configuration are required before any optional tracker is activated. No GA4 tag, CMP or cookieless ping is included now. A self-hosted alternative could be assessed later; R1 avoids an external analytics vendor.

Define funnel units as distinct projects/leads/engagements rather than raw clicks. Segment by project type and acquisition source only after an approved minimal attribution design. Use actual service-effort and cash collection data to evaluate contribution margin. Do not invent targets, success rates or experiments; first establish a baseline and one measurable hypothesis.

Privacy design references: [CAI necessity/consent guidance](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees/consentement-personnes-entreprises), [CAI privacy assessment guide](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/uploads/pdfs/CAI_GU_EFVP.pdf?gt=obligation). These inform design; they are not a legal opinion or completed assessment.
